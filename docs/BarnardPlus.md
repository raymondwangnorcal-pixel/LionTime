# Barnard Buildings to Add

Buildings and facilities at Barnard College to integrate into LionTime.

## Library

**Barnard Library (Milstein Center)**
- Location: 3009 Broadway (Milstein Center)
- Summer hours: Mon–Fri 9 AM–9 PM, Sat–Sun 9 AM–6 PM (study spaces); circulation desk Mon–Fri 10 AM–6 PM
- CU/BC ID swipe required for building entry
- Already tracked by Columbia's hours API at `hours.library.columbia.edu/locations/barnard`
- Note: Barnard Fitness Center (LeFrak Center) is already in LionTime under Fitness

## Dining

**Hewitt Dining**
- Location: Barnard Hall, basement
- Typical hours: breakfast, lunch, dinner blocks (varies by day)
- Main Barnard dining hall; published on `dineoncampus.com/barnard` (403s on scrape)

**Liz's Place**
- Location: Diana Center, 1st floor
- Typical hours: Mon–Fri ~8 AM–5:30 PM, Sat ~8 AM–5 PM
- Grab-and-go café

**Diana Center Cafe**
- Location: Diana Center, 2nd floor
- Typical hours: Mon–Fri ~8 AM–3 PM, closed weekends

**Bubble Tea & Sushi**
- Location: Milstein Center, 1st floor
- Typical hours: Mon–Fri ~11 AM–5 PM, closed weekends

**LeFrak Byte Kiosk**
- Location: Barnard Hall, 1st floor
- Hours: 24/7
- Vending/micro-market
